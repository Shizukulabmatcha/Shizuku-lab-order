Shizuku Lab V30 — two-stage customer confirmation emails

Upload the complete ZIP to Vercel.

Customer email flow:
1. Placing an order alone does not send the customer an email.
2. After payment proof is submitted, the customer receives “We received your order” while payment is under review.
3. After payment is marked Paid/Confirmed in Admin, the customer receives “Your order is confirmed”.

Both customer templates are editable in Admin > Notifications.
The owner continues to receive the enabled new-order and payment-proof notifications.
